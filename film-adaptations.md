# From Page to Screen: The a16z Infra Reading List Adaptations

### Sci-Fi: Must Reads (S Tier)
* **Dune series by Frank Herbert**
    * [Dune (1984)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0087182/) – Movie directed by David Lynch
    * [Frank Herbert's Dune (2000)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0142032/) – Sci-Fi Channel miniseries
    * [Frank Herbert's Children of Dune (2003)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0285331/) – Sci-Fi Channel miniseries
    * [Dune: Part One (2021)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1160419/) – Movie directed by Denis Villeneuve
    * [Dune: Part Two (2024)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt15239678/) – Movie directed by Denis Villeneuve
    * [Dune: Prophecy (2024)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt10466740/) – HBO Max series

* **Philip K. Dick**
    * *The Minority Report*: [Minority Report (2002 movie)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0181689/) and [Minority Report (2015 TV series)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt4053092/)
    * *The Man in the High Castle*: [The Man in the High Castle (2015–2019 Amazon series)](https://www.amazon.com/The-Man-In-High-Castle-Season-1/dp/B00RSGIVVO)
    * *We Can Remember It For You Wholesale*: [Total Recall (1990 movie)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0100802/), [Total Recall 2070 (1999 TV series)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0183882/), and [Total Recall (2012 movie)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1386703/)
    * *Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?*: [Blade Runner (1982 movie)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0083658/), [Blade Runner 2049 (2017 movie)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1856101/), [Blade Runner: Black Lotus (2021 anime series)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt9359796/), and the upcoming [Blade Runner 2099 (Amazon series)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt18257018/)

* **Sprawl trilogy by William Gibson**
    * *Neuromancer*: [Upcoming Apple TV+ series](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/neuromancer/umc.cmc.2b2n0m8n9n6n8n9n0n2n4n6n8)

* **The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy series by Douglas Adams**
    * [The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy (1981)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0081874/) – BBC TV series
    * [The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy (2005)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0371724/) – Movie

* **Ender's Saga by Orson Scott Card**
    * [Ender's Game (2013)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1731141/) – Movie
