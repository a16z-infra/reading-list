In his early work, Stephenson feels like a sci fi descendant of William Gibson. Many of these books were cyberpunk-ish and explored themes around the metaverse, distributed computing, and biological augmentation. His more recent work, e.g. *Anathem*, is breaking out of this mold and is very relevant to current discussions around AI. All of Stephenson's work is hugely imaginative and includes truly wild ideas, like the Drummers (a cult living in a cave that collectively performs large-scale computations through hallucinogenic drugs and endless sex) and cryptocurrency. At their best, Stephenson's books are diligently researched, thought provoking, and funny. Fair warning, some people find the endings to some of these books unsatisfying.

  - [*The Diamond Age*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Diamond_Age) (1995)

  - [*Anathem*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anathem) (2008)
